<div>

<p>We’re thrilled to share that <strong>Dat Ecosystem</strong> has officially joined the <a href="https://www.apereo.org">Apereo Foundation</a> as a <strong>fiscally sponsored project</strong>. 🎉</p>

<p>This marks a significant milestone in our journey to build and maintain <strong>open peer-to-peer infrastructure</strong> for a more decentralized and resilient web. By becoming part of Apereo, the Dat Ecosystem gains a trusted nonprofit home — allowing us to strengthen governance, receive tax-deductible donations, and focus our collective energy on building technology and community.</p>

<blockquote>
<p>“This collaboration gives the Dat community the stability and support it needs to keep pushing forward open, peer-to-peer infrastructure for the web,” said members of the Dat Ecosystem.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>Apereo has a long history of supporting impactful open-source projects in education and beyond. Through this partnership, Dat Ecosystem will be better positioned to collaborate across communities, grow sustainably, and ensure the long-term stewardship of the protocols and tools that power our shared infrastructure.</p>

<h2>Why Apereo?</h2>
<p>Apereo is a respected nonprofit foundation supporting a range of open-source projects in education and technology. Their fiscal sponsorship model allows projects like Dat Ecosystem to operate under a shared nonprofit umbrella, while retaining their autonomy and community-driven values.</p>

<p>With Apereo as our fiscal sponsor, the Dat Ecosystem can:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Receive and manage funds transparently</li>
  <li>Ensure organizational resilience</li>
  <li>Foster collaboration across open-source communities</li>
  <li>Focus on building tools instead of bureaucracy</li>
</ul>
